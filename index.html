Frequently Asked Questions

Answers before you get started

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes. Reach out with your project details and location, and we will schedule a site visit or phone consultation to prepare a free, no-obligation quote.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines depend on scope and size. A standard residential build typically runs several months, while smaller renovation work can be completed in a matter of weeks. We confirm a realistic timeline before work begins.

Do you assist with permits and approvals?

We guide clients through the documentation and approvals required for their specific project and location, working alongside relevant authorities where needed.

What areas do you cover?

We are based in Nairobi and serve the greater Nairobi area, including Kilimani, Karen, Kiambu, Ruaka and surrounding towns. We are open to projects further afield on request.

How do I get started?

Message us on WhatsApp or call 0726 066474 with a short description of your project. We will arrange a consultation and take it from there.
